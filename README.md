### 🚀 Projects

#### 🔔 Digital Pager System
A web-based replacement for the physical pagers used in restaurants and cafés. Customers follow their order on their own phone and get notified the moment it's ready, with no extra hardware.
- **Problem:** Physical pagers are costly, get lost, and limit where customers can wait
- **UX focus:** Zero-install flow: scan a QR code, see your order status, get notified
- **Stack:** HTML5 · JavaScript · Node.js

#### 💳 Nuqta (نقطة): Cloud POS System
A multi-tenant point-of-sale SaaS for restaurants and cafés, with the digital pager built in.
- iPad-friendly cashier screen designed with HCI principles to **speed up service and reduce input errors** in busy environments
- Live orders / kitchen board, QR menu for customers, and simplified tax invoices with QR codes
- Data isolation between businesses using PostgreSQL **Row Level Security**
- **Stack:** Next.js 14 · JavaScript · PostgreSQL

#### 🚗 Mawqif (موقف): Smart Parking App
An app that helps drivers find and access parking with less friction.
- **UX focus:** Usability and a clear, simple user flow for a problem drivers face every day

#### 🎬 CraveLab: Branding & Content
A creative project covering brand identity and marketing video production, combining business and design thinking with the technical side.
